
# Generative AI Community Insights

## About This Project

I'm interested in the intersection of generative AI, human behavior, and community growth.

My background in psychology, consumer technology marketing, and international business has shaped how I think about technology adoption: people don't engage with products simply because they're powerful. They engage when they understand the possibilities, see meaningful applications, and feel motivated to participate.

## Areas I'm Exploring

- **Creator Discovery:** How people discover new AI models and creative tools.
- **Community Engagement:** What encourages experimentation, knowledge sharing, and sustained participation.
- **User Adoption:** How education, onboarding, and peer learning influence product adoption.
- **Community Feedback:** How creators' experiences can inform better products and developer resources.

## Current Focus

I'm exploring generative AI platforms, creator workflows, and the relationship between community participation and product growth.

This is an evolving collection of observations, ideas, and practical explorations.
